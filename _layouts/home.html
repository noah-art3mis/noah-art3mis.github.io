What is AI slop?

Master’s dissertation, London Interdisciplinary School, 2025

An interdisciplinary study of low-quality AI content: how people describe it, the forms it takes, and what distinguishes it from human writing.

Combining thematic analysis, topic modelling, and statistical linguistics, I found slop news to be less varied and more positive than human-written, high-quality news.
